Common Questions

Straight answers before you call

How much does a new roof cost in West Michigan?

Most residential roof replacements in the Grand Rapids and Lowell area fall in a wide range depending on the size of the roof, the pitch, the material, and how much decking needs to be replaced. The honest answer is that no reputable roofer can price your roof without seeing it. We give you a free, written estimate after a real inspection, with no pressure and no surprise add-ons.

Do you help with insurance claims for storm damage?

Yes. Wind, hail, and ice damage are often covered by your homeowners insurance. We inspect the roof for free, document the damage the way an adjuster needs to see it, meet with your adjuster, and walk you through the whole claim. You do not have to fight your insurer alone.

Are you licensed, insured, and certified?

Yes. 3 Peaks is a CertainTeed Pro Installer, BBB A+ accredited, and veteran-owned. Our work is backed by a lifetime craftsmanship warranty and a 10-year workmanship warranty, on top of the manufacturer's material warranty.

What areas do you serve?

We are based in Lowell and serve all of West Michigan, including Ada, Cascade, Forest Hills, Grand Rapids, Grandville, Kentwood, Wyoming, Rockford, Caledonia, Byron Center, Hudsonville, Ionia, Saranac, and the surrounding towns.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. We offer fair pricing and flexible financing so a new roof or exterior project fits your budget. We will go over the options with you during your free estimate.
