Seamless gutters custom-fit to your home and sized for the snowmelt and ice a Michigan winter throws at them.

Why gutters matter more in Michigan

A gutter system does one job: get water away from your roof, siding, and foundation before it causes damage. In West Michigan that job gets harder every winter, when ice dams back water under shingles and heavy snowmelt overwhelms undersized or damaged gutters. A properly sized, properly pitched system:

  • Keeps water away from your foundation, reducing leaks and structural damage
  • Protects landscaping and siding from erosion and staining
  • Reduces the risk of basement flooding from water pooling at the foundation
  • Cuts down on the ice dams that damage eaves and shingles in January

  • Material options including aluminum, steel, copper, and vinyl to match your home and budget
  • Downspout installation and placement to route water safely away from the foundation

Sized to your roof, not a standard chart

Gutters are part of your roof's drainage system, not an accessory bolted on after the fact. The same crew that roofs your house sizes the trough and downspout count to your actual roof pitch and square footage, so the system can carry what a heavy Michigan storm or a fast spring melt sends down it. That installation carries our lifetime craftsmanship warranty, and financing is available.

Signs it is time for new gutters

Not sure if a repair will hold or you need a full replacement? Watch for cracks or splits, peeling paint or rust spots, water pooling near the foundation, sagging or detached sections, and frequent clogs despite cleaning. A free inspection will tell you which one you are looking at.

Gutter Installation FAQ

Questions homeowners ask us

How long does gutter installation take?

Most homes are done in one day, depending on the size of the property and the complexity of the job.

What are the best materials for gutters?

Aluminum is the most common choice for its durability, affordability, and rust resistance. Copper and steel are also good options for longevity and looks. We will help you pick the right one for your home and budget.

Do gutter guards really work?

Yes. Gutter guards reduce how much debris enters your gutters, which cuts down on maintenance and extends how long the system lasts.

How do I know if my gutters need replacing?

Cracks, leaks, sagging, or water pooling near your foundation are all signs it may be time. A free inspection will tell you whether a repair or full replacement is the right call.
