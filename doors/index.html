Your front door is the largest moving air seal in the house, and how well it holds heat and keeps people out comes down to the weatherstripping quality and the door's U-value, not how it looks in a catalog photo. A door installation from 3 Peaks is a full removal-and-replace job, measured, sealed, and hung to actually perform through a Michigan winter, backed by our lifetime craftsmanship warranty. When it is time for a new door

A door does double duty as security and insulation. It is usually time to replace, not just repair, when you see:

  • Drafts or daylight around the frame when it is shut
  • Sticking, warping, or trouble locking properly
  • Rot, cracking, or damage from age or weather
  • Rising energy bills you cannot explain another way
  • A door that no longer matches how you want your entry to look

Doors we install

  • Entry doors: wood, steel, or fiberglass, for curb appeal and security
  • Patio and sliding doors: French, sliding glass, and bi-fold options for indoor-outdoor flow
  • Garage doors: sectional, roll-up, and custom options, insulated or not
  • Interior doors: panel, flush, glass, and barn styles
