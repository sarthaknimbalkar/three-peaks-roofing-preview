Gutter repair in Lowell & West Michigan

Sagging sections, leaks, and pulled fasteners fixed before they turn into foundation or roof damage.

Most gutter repair calls in Michigan trace back to the same mechanical failure: fasteners pulled loose and sections sagging under the weight of trapped ice and wet leaves, a stress this climate puts on gutters that milder states rarely see. 3 Peaks fixes those pulled sections, along with leaks, clogs, and broken downspouts, so the system actually drains again, with a free inspection and written estimate before any work starts. We are veteran-owned and match repairs to your existing gutters.

Why a small gutter problem does not stay small

A sagging or leaking gutter section is not just cosmetic. Left alone, it lets water pool where it should be draining away, which leads to:

  • Water seeping into your foundation, basement, or walls
  • Roof rot and leaks from water backing up under the drip edge
  • Pest problems from standing water attracting mosquitoes and rodents
  • Landscape erosion from water overflowing where it should be channeled
  • A shorter lifespan for your whole roofing and drainage system

What every gutter repair covers

  • Gutter leak repairs using professional-grade sealants or patching for cracks and holes
  • Realignment and re-attachment for sagging or loose sections pulling away from the roofline
  • Downspout repair or replacement so water flows away from the foundation instead of pooling
  • Clog removal to clear the debris causing the overflow in the first place
  • Rust and corrosion treatment for metal gutters showing wear
  • Seamless gutter repair that keeps a smooth, uniform look on seamless systems

Signs it is time to call

Water spilling over the edges, sagging or detached sections, visible cracks or rust spots, pooling water near the foundation, and mold or peeling paint near the gutter line are all signs worth a call. Waiting on these usually means a bigger repair, or a full replacement, later.

No handoff if a repair turns into more

The crew fixing a pulled section is the same one that installs full systems, so if an inspection turns up more damage than expected, like a run that needs full replacement, you are not starting over with a new company. That coverage extends to a lifetime craftsmanship warranty on the repair itself, and financing is available for larger jobs.

Gutter Repair FAQ

Questions homeowners ask us

How often should I have my gutters inspected?

Twice a year, in spring and fall, catches most problems before they turn into a bigger repair.

What are the most common causes of gutter damage?

Clogged gutters, weather damage, rust and corrosion, loose fasteners, and improper original installation are the most common causes. Regular maintenance helps prevent most of them.

Can I repair my gutters myself?

Basic cleaning can be a DIY job, but leak, alignment, or structural repairs are safer and longer-lasting when a professional handles them.

Do you offer gutter replacement if repair is not worth it?

Yes. If your gutters are damaged beyond a cost-effective repair, we install a full replacement system so your home stays protected.
