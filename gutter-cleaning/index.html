Gutter cleaning in Lowell & West Michigan

Clogged gutters cause roof leaks and foundation problems long before anyone notices the leaves. We clear, flush, and check the whole system.

Clogged gutters are not a cosmetic problem in Michigan. Water backs up under the roof edge and freezes into ice dams over winter, and overflow in the meantime rots fascia board and stains siding. Gutter cleaning from 3 Peaks clears leaves and debris from your gutters and downspouts, flushes the system to confirm it actually drains, and checks for cracks, loose brackets, or sagging while the crew is already up there. We are veteran-owned, and every visit comes with a free, no-obligation estimate for any repairs we find.

Why clogged gutters are worse than they look

Gutters that overflow are not just messy, they push water where it does not belong. Left neglected, clogged gutters lead to:

  • Water seeping under shingles and causing roof rot and leaks
  • Foundation cracks from water eroding the soil at the base of your home
  • Mold and mildew growth from trapped moisture
  • Pest problems from standing water attracting mosquitoes and rodents
  • Landscape erosion from water overflowing onto beds and plantings

In Michigan, wet leaves in fall and heavy spring runoff make the problem worse than in drier climates, which is why regular cleaning matters more here.

What every gutter cleaning includes

  • Gutter inspection to assess debris buildup and check for cracks, loose brackets, or sagging
  • Debris removal by hand or with tools to clear leaves, twigs, and dirt
  • Downspout clearing so water flows freely instead of backing up
  • Gutter flushing with water to confirm drainage is working
  • On-the-spot minor repairs like tightening brackets or sealing small leaks
  • Recommendations for anything that needs a larger repair

How often gutters need cleaning in West Michigan

Most homes do well with cleaning twice a year, in spring and fall. Homes with heavy tree coverage may need quarterly visits, and any home should get an extra check after a major storm. Keeping to that schedule is what prevents the expensive repairs.

A sagging bracket or a downspout ready to fail does not get handed off to someone else, it gets fixed on the spot or scheduled with the same roofing and gutter crew that already knows the house. Financing is available for larger repair work, and every job is backed by our craftsmanship warranty.

Gutter Cleaning FAQ

Questions homeowners ask us

How often should I have my gutters cleaned?

Twice a year, in spring and fall, works for most homes. If you have a lot of surrounding trees or heavy storms, more frequent cleaning may be worth it.

What are the signs my gutters need cleaning?

Water overflow, sagging gutters, plant growth inside the gutters, pests, and water stains on siding or the foundation are all common signs.

Can I clean my gutters myself?

You can, but ladder work is genuinely dangerous, and a DIY clean often misses cracks or loose brackets a trained eye catches. Hiring it out gets you safety and a full inspection at the same time.

Do you offer maintenance plans for regular gutter cleaning?

Yes. We offer scheduled cleanings based on your home's needs so gutters stay clear year-round instead of getting caught up on once things back up.
