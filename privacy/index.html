Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, and how we keep it safe.

Last updated: 2026

What we collect

When you use a contact form, schedule a free inspection, or call the number on this site, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, property address, and any message you send us. We also collect basic analytics about how visitors use the site, such as which pages are viewed, to understand what is useful and what is not.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to your inspection or repair request, schedule and complete your project, follow up about an estimate, and answer questions. We use site analytics to improve the pages and make the site easier to use.

We do not sell your information

We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone, for any reason.

Who we share it with

We share your information only with the service providers we need to run the business, such as our scheduling and customer relationship tools and our email provider, so they can help us contact you and manage your project. We do not share your information with unrelated third parties for marketing.

Cookies and analytics

This site may use basic cookies and analytics tools to see how pages are used and to measure whether our advertising is working. You can disable cookies in your browser settings; the site will still work, though some features may be limited.

How long we keep your information

We keep contact and project information for as long as needed to complete your project, honor any warranty, and meet our own recordkeeping and tax requirements, and we remove it when it is no longer needed for those purposes.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete the information we have on file for you at any time by calling or emailing us using the contact details below.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.
