When it is time for new windows

Old windows cost you money every month they sit drafty. It is usually time for replacement, not another repair, when you notice:

  • Drafts around the frame even when closed
  • Windows that are hard to open, close, or lock
  • Condensation building up between the panes
  • Rising heating or cooling bills without another explanation
  • Visible cracks, rot, or warping in the frame

A free estimate will tell you plainly whether your windows can be repaired or if replacement is the better investment.

Window FAQ

Questions homeowners ask us

How do I know when it is time to replace my windows?

Drafts, difficulty opening or closing, condensation between panes, high energy bills, and visible cracks or rot are the main signs. A free inspection will confirm what you are looking at.

Are energy-efficient windows worth it?

Yes. Energy-efficient windows lower heating and cooling costs, improve comfort, and add to your home's value, especially through a Michigan winter.

What window material is best for my home?

It depends on your needs. Vinyl is low maintenance and affordable, wood offers classic beauty, fiberglass is durable, and aluminum works well for modern designs. We help you decide at the estimate.

Do you offer a warranty on windows?

Yes. Every window installation carries our lifetime craftsmanship warranty and a 10-year workmanship warranty for your peace of mind.
