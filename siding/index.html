When it is time for new siding

Siding takes a beating in West Michigan between summer sun, winter ice, and everything in between. It is usually time to replace rather than patch when you see:

  • Cracking, warping, or fading across large sections
  • Mold, mildew, or moisture stains that keep coming back
  • Rising energy bills from poor insulation behind the panels
  • Siding pulling away from the house or rattling in wind
  • Damage from a storm, impact, or general age

A free inspection will tell you straight whether a repair holds or a full replacement is the smarter move.

Siding materials we install

  • Vinyl siding: affordable, low maintenance, resistant to moisture, rot, and pests
  • Fiber cement siding: durable, fire and weather resistant, mimics the look of wood
  • Wood siding: a classic look that needs periodic maintenance
  • Metal siding: modern, highly durable, resistant to fire, insects, and rot
  • Composite siding: resists mold and cracking with good insulation properties

Siding FAQ

Questions homeowners ask us

How long does siding installation take?

Most homes are done in about 1 to 2 weeks depending on size and material. We will give you a real timeline at the estimate, not a guess.

What are the signs my siding needs to be replaced?

Cracking, warping, fading, mold growth, and rising energy bills are the main signs. If you notice any of these, it is worth a free inspection to see what is really going on.

What is the most durable siding material?

Fiber cement and metal siding are among the most durable options for West Michigan weather, standing up well to storms, fire risk, and pests.

Do you offer a warranty on siding work?

Yes. Every siding job carries our lifetime craftsmanship warranty and a 10-year workmanship warranty, covering the panels, seams, and flashing we install.
