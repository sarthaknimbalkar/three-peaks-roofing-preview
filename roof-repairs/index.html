Signs your roof needs a repair, not a wait-and-see

Roof damage rarely announces itself where it starts. A ceiling stain is usually downstream of the actual leak, which is why we check the attic as well as the shingles. Call for an inspection if you see:

  • Water leaking through the ceiling or staining drywall
  • Cracked, curling, or missing shingles after wind or hail
  • Sagging roof sections or exposed underlayment
  • Failed flashing or sealant around chimneys, vents, or skylights
  • A fallen limb or other debris impact

Not sure if it is a repair or a full replacement? The inspection tells you with evidence, not a sales pitch.

Emergency repairs when a leak cannot wait

An active leak during a storm, a tree limb through the decking, or shingles stripped off with underlayment exposed get worse by the hour. We treat these as priority calls: fast response, emergency tarping or temporary sealing to stop the damage, then a permanent repair once the roof is dry enough to work safely.

Storm damage and insurance claims

If wind, hail, or a storm caused the damage, a repair may be covered by your homeowners insurance. We document the damage with photos, meet your adjuster on the roof, and check the insurance report against actual measurements before any work starts. Start with a free inspection.

Roof Repair FAQ

Questions homeowners ask us

How do I know if my roof needs repairs?

Common signs include water leaks, missing shingles, sagging sections, mold growth, and visible damage after storms. If you notice any of these, schedule an inspection and we will give you a straight answer.

Do you offer emergency roof repairs?

Yes. Emergency repairs are treated as priority calls. We respond as fast as conditions allow, starting with emergency tarping to stop active damage, then schedule the permanent fix.

Should I repair my roof or replace it?

If the damage is localized, the roof is under about 15 years old, and the decking is sound, repair is usually the right call. Widespread granule loss, repeated leaks in different spots, or a roof near the end of its lifespan point to replacement. The inspection answers this with evidence.

Will storm damage repair be covered by insurance?

If your roof was damaged by wind, hail, or a storm, repair is often covered by homeowners insurance. We document the damage, meet your adjuster on site, and check the report against actual measurements before work starts.
