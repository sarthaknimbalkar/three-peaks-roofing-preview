When it is time for a new roof

Most asphalt roofs in West Michigan last 15 to 25 years, but our winters push them hard. It is usually time for a full replacement, not another patch, when you see:

  • Shingles curling, cracking, or missing across large areas
  • Granules filling your gutters (your shingles are wearing out)
  • Daylight, stains, or sagging in the attic
  • Leaks in more than one spot, or a roof past 20 years old
  • Storm, hail, or wind damage your insurance may cover

Not sure which you are looking at? That is what the free inspection is for. We will tell you straight whether a repair will hold or a replacement is the smarter money.

Roof Installation FAQ

Questions homeowners ask us

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most homes are torn off and re-roofed in a single day. Larger or steeper roofs, or ones that need a lot of decking replaced, can take two. We start early, work clean, and haul everything away when we are done.

What roofing materials do you install?

We are a CertainTeed Pro Installer and install their full asphalt shingle line, which covers the look and durability most West Michigan homes need. We will help you pick the right shingle for your home and budget during the estimate.

Will my new roof be covered by insurance?

If your roof was damaged by wind, hail, or a storm, a replacement is often covered by your homeowners policy. We inspect for free, document the damage, and work directly with your adjuster so you get what you are owed.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes. Every roof we install carries our lifetime craftsmanship warranty and a 10-year workmanship warranty, on top of the CertainTeed material warranty covering the shingles themselves.
