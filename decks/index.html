Deck builders in Lowell, MI

Custom decks built to hold up through Michigan freeze-thaw seasons. Veteran-owned and backed for life.

Most Michigan deck failures start where nobody looks: the ledger board, where the deck attaches to the house, letting water in behind the flashing and rotting the framing from the inside while the decking on top still looks fine. Freeze-thaw cycling on the footings does the rest. A deck from 3 Peaks gets built or rebuilt around getting that connection right, from a simple platform to a multi-level design. We give you a free, written estimate, handle the permits, and back the work with our lifetime craftsmanship warranty.

When it is time for a new or rebuilt deck

Michigan winters are hard on outdoor structures. It is usually time for a rebuild, not just a repair, when you notice:

  • Loose, cracked, or soft boards
  • Rust or wear on fasteners and railings
  • Standing water that will not drain
  • A structure that no longer feels sturdy underfoot
  • A deck that has never matched how you actually use your yard

Deck types we build

  • Wood decks: cedar, redwood, or pressure-treated lumber for a classic look
  • Composite decks: low maintenance, resistant to rot, splinters, and insects
  • PVC decks: highly durable, resistant to moisture, no staining required
  • Multi-level and wraparound decks: for larger yards or more complex layouts

What a 3 Peaks deck project includes

  • Consultation and design around your budget and how you use your yard
  • Site preparation and permits handled for you to meet local codes
  • Construction and installation by our own trained crew
  • Finishing touches like railings and lighting
  • Final inspection before we call the job complete

Deck FAQ

Questions homeowners ask us

What is the best material for a low-maintenance deck?

Composite and PVC decking resist rot, splinters, and fading, making them the best choice for homeowners who want to spend less time on upkeep.

How long does it take to build a deck?

Most standard decks take 1 to 3 weeks depending on size and materials. Larger or multi-level decks can take longer. We give you a real timeline at the estimate.

Do I need a permit to build a deck?

Yes, most deck projects require a permit to meet local building codes. We handle all the necessary permits for you.

Do you offer a warranty on decks?

Yes. Every deck we build carries our lifetime craftsmanship warranty and a 10-year workmanship warranty.
