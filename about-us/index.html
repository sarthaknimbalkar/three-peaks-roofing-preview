Meet the veteran-owned team behind 3 Peaks

A small crew that treats your home like our own, backed by the resources to get the job done right.

3 Peaks Roofing & Exteriors was founded by Mitch Cusack, a veteran, on one idea: you should not have to choose between the care of a small local crew and the strength of a big company. With 3 Peaks you get both, the same people from the first handshake to the final cleanup, backed by a lifetime craftsmanship warranty and a ten-year workmanship warranty on every job.

Why Mitch started 3 Peaks

Mitch Cusack built 3 Peaks Roofing & Exteriors around a small, dedicated team that could give homeowners the personal attention only a close-knit crew provides, while still carrying the resources and know-how of a big company. That means you get a real person who answers the phone, a crew that shows up when they say they will, and a company big enough to handle a full roof replacement, gutters, siding, windows, doors, or a deck without passing you off to a subcontractor.

How much we care

Good roofing work is not just about shingles. It is about how a company treats people. 3 Peaks is community-driven, and the team takes pride in the neighborhoods it serves across Lowell and West Michigan. Every project runs low-pressure and informed, with competitive pricing and honest timelines, so you know what is happening and why.

If insurance is involved, you are not left to sort it out on your own. The team helps with the claim, meets with your adjuster, and walks you through the process step by step. That is how 3 Peaks shows how much it cares, not just in the sales pitch, but in the follow-through.
