Skylights sealed right the first time by a crew that already knows your roof. Veteran-owned and backed for life.

Skylight glass almost never fails. What fails is the flashing where it meets the roofline, especially after a Michigan winter's freeze-thaw cycling has worked at the seal all season. A skylight job from 3 Peaks is an installation, leak repair, or full replacement handled by our own roofing crew, built around getting that flashing detail right the first time. We give you a free inspection and written estimate, and back the work with our lifetime craftsmanship warranty.

When it is time to install or replace a skylight

Skylights are only as good as their seal. It is time to call when you notice:

  • Leaks, staining, or water marks around the skylight
  • Cracked, fogged, or cloudy glass
  • Drafts or condensation building up around the frame
  • A skylight older than 20 years that has never been serviced
  • You simply want more natural light in a room

Skylight services we offer

  • Skylight installation in new construction or retrofitted into an existing roof
  • Leak detection and repair to find and fix water intrusion before it spreads
  • Glass replacement for cracked or fogged panes
  • Sealing and flashing repair to stop leaks at the source
  • Full replacement with modern, energy-efficient models when a unit is beyond repair

Why a roofing crew matters for skylights

Most skylight leaks trace back to bad flashing, not the skylight itself. Because we are roofers first, we understand how the skylight, the flashing, and the surrounding shingles all have to work together to keep water out. That is the difference between a skylight that leaks again next spring and one that does not.

The flashing is covered, not just the unit

A skylight job carries our lifetime craftsmanship warranty and a 10-year workmanship warranty on the whole installation, flashing included, since that is the part most likely to be the actual problem. A skylight job pairs easily with a roof inspection or replacement in the same visit.

Most skylight installations take one to two days depending on the type and how much work the roof needs around it.

Skylights can be installed on most roof types, including flat, pitched, and metal roofs. We assess your roof structure to recommend the right solution.

Proper installation, quality flashing, and routine maintenance are what actually prevent leaks. If you already have one, call us for a prompt repair.
